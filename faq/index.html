Products

What are your products? +
ELVYN Labs offers research-grade peptides including BPC-157, TB-500, GHK-Cu, and GLO — our proprietary blend. All products are formulated for in vitro research use only and are not intended for human consumption.
Are your products safe for human use? +
Our products are strictly for research use only and are not approved for human consumption. They have not been evaluated by the FDA for therapeutic, diagnostic, or clinical use. See our Research Use Only disclaimer for full details.
What purity levels can I expect? +
All ELVYN Labs peptides are produced to high research-grade purity standards. Certificates of Analysis (CoA) are available upon request. Contact us if you need documentation for your research.
How should I store the products? +
Lyophilized (freeze-dried) peptides should be stored in a cool, dry place away from direct light. For long-term storage, keep at -20°C. Once reconstituted, store at 2–8°C and use within a reasonable timeframe as noted in product documentation.
