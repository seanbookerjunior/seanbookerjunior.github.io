GHK-Cu

Copper Peptide · Cellular Regeneration

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper peptide complex that declines significantly with age. Research shows it activates over 4,000 human genes and plays a role in wound healing, anti-inflammatory signaling, antioxidant activity, and collagen synthesis. It has been extensively studied for its regenerative effects on skin, hair follicles, and connective tissue.
