BPC-157

Body Protection Compound · Tissue Repair & Recovery

BPC-157 is a synthetic peptide derived from a protein found in human gastric juice. Research suggests it plays a role in accelerating wound healing, supporting tendon and ligament repair, and reducing inflammation. It has been studied extensively in animal models for its regenerative properties across musculoskeletal and gastrointestinal tissue.
